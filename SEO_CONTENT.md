## What is Consent Form Builder?

The Consent Form Builder is a free, browser-based drag-and-drop tool that lets tattoo and piercing professionals create custom consultation and waiver forms. The tool generates clean HTML5 forms with client-side validation, digital signature capture, and PDF export. All data processing happens locally in the browser - no client information is stored on any server.

The builder includes a field palette with 10 field types (text, email, phone, date, textarea, checkbox, radio, dropdown, header, signature), a properties editor for customizing each field's label and validation rules, and a template library with 7 pre-built forms for common studio scenarios. The conditional logic engine automatically shows or hides fields based on client responses, and the medical history system provides standardized health screening questions for piercing and tattoo procedures.

## Who Should Use This

## How to Use

**Step 1 - Load a Template or Start Blank**
The tool loads with a default template. Click the template selector to choose from 7 pre-built forms: Standard Tattoo Consultation, Standard Piercing Consultation, Large Piece / Multi-Session, Minor Consent Form, Cover-Up Consultation, Touch-Up Consultation, or Blank Template.

**Step 2 - Drag Fields from the Palette**
The left panel displays 10 field types. Click and drag any field onto the center canvas. The canvas accepts fields from the palette and allows reordering within sections.

**Step 3 - Configure Field Properties**
Click any field on the canvas to open the properties editor in the right panel. Edit the field label, placeholder text, and toggle the required checkbox. Click "Update" to save changes.

**Step 4 - Reorder Fields on the Canvas**
Drag fields within the canvas to change their order. Each section can contain multiple fields, and sections themselves can be reorganized.

**Step 5 - Preview the Form**
Click the preview button to see how the form will appear to clients. Fill in test data to verify conditional logic and validation rules work correctly.

**Step 6 - Export as PDF or Embed**
Click "Generate PDF" to download a formatted PDF containing all client responses and their digital signature. Use the Embed tab to copy an iframe code for your studio website.

## Use Cases

### Use Case 1 - Piercing Studio Intake

A piercing studio uses the Standard Piercing Consultation template. The form includes:

- **Client Information**: Full name, date of birth, email, phone number
- **Medical History**: Allergies (latex, metals, adhesives), blood thinners status, pregnancy screening
- **Piercing Details**: Location (e.g., "Left nostril"), jewelry preference (e.g., "Titanium labret")
- **Consent**: Age verification checkbox, risk acknowledgment, aftercare agreement
- **Signature**: Client signs on a tablet using the signature pad

The artist reviews the medical history, sees "No" for blood thinners and "Not applicable" for pregnancy, then proceeds with the piercing. The PDF is saved to the client file.

### Use Case 2 - Tattoo Cover-Up Consultation

A client wants to cover an old tattoo on their forearm. The artist uses the Cover-Up Consultation template:

- **Existing Tattoo**: "Black tribal band, 8 years old, faded significantly"
- **Cover-Up Goals**: "Dark floral sleeve, flexible with size and placement"
- **Medical History**: Client checks "Yes" for keloid scarring tendency
- **Consent**: Client checks "I am flexible with size and placement to ensure coverage"

The artist notes the keloid concern and adjusts the aftercare plan accordingly. The PDF is generated with the client's signature and design notes.

### Use Case 3 - Minor Client with Guardian Consent

A 16-year-old wants a cartilage piercing. The studio uses the Minor Consent Form template:

- **Minor Information**: Name, date of birth
- **Guardian Information**: Parent name, relationship ("Mother"), ID type ("Driver's License")
- **Guardian Consent**: Legal guardian certification checkbox, procedure consent checkbox
- **Signatures**: Guardian signs first, then minor signs (optional)

Both signatures are captured on the signature pad. The PDF includes both signatures and the guardian's ID information for legal compliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Are the consent forms legally binding?**
These templates follow industry standards for tattoo and piercing studios. We recommend having your specific forms reviewed by local legal counsel to ensure compliance with regional regulations.

**Q2: Can I add my studio logo to the form?**
The current version does not include a logo upload feature. The PDF generator uses the form name as the header. Studio branding can be added by editing the PDF generation code or by overlaying a logo after download.

**Q3: Do you store my clients' data?**
No. All data processing happens entirely in the browser. No client information is sent to any server, stored in cookies, or logged. The PDF is generated locally and saved directly to your device.

**Q4: Is there a limit to how many forms I can build?**
No. This is a free, unlimited resource. You can create as many custom forms as needed, save them locally, and reuse templates.

**Q5: Does the form work on tablets?**
Yes. The builder and preview are fully responsive. Clients can sign directly on a tablet using the signature pad, which works with touch input.

**Q6: Can I embed this form on my studio website?**
Yes. Click the "Embed Code" tab to copy an iframe snippet. Paste it into your website's HTML to display the form builder or a specific form preview on your site.

**Q7: What field types are available for medical screening?**
The medical history system includes checkbox groups for allergies (latex, metals, antibiotics), medical conditions (diabetes, hemophilia, heart conditions), radio buttons for blood thinners and pregnancy status, and text areas for current medications.

**Q8: How does conditional logic work?**
Fields can be configured to show or hide based on previous responses. For example, if a client selects "Yes" for a medical condition, additional detail fields appear. The logic engine supports equals, not equals, contains, greater than, and less than operators.

**Q9: Can I save my form and edit it later?**
The current version does not include cloud save functionality. You can save the HTML source of your form or export the PDF. For repeated use, embed the form on your website or reload a template.

**Q10: What file format does the PDF export use?**
The PDF is generated using jsPDF library and exports as a standard PDF file. It includes the form title, date, all client responses organized by section, and the digital signature image.
