# Studio Consultation Form Builder - Template Library Guide

The **Poli International Studio Consultation Form Builder (Tool #13)** includes 8 production-grade templates tailored for modern body art studios, health department compliance, physician authorizations, and legal consent requirements.

---

## 📚 Included Templates Reference

### 1. 💉 Tattoo Consent & Release Agreement (`tattoo_consent`)
- **Primary Use**: Standard daily tattoo appointments for adult clients.
- **Key Sections**:
  - Studio & Tattooist Information (health permit, workstation ID, artist licensing).
  - Client Personal Identification (full legal name, government ID, emergency contact).
  - Comprehensive Medical History (allergies, diabetes, epilepsy, blood thinners, skin conditions).
  - Mandatory Physician Clearance Clause (conditional medical authorization).
  - Design & Placement Specifications (stencil approval, anatomical location, dimensions).
  - Legal Waivers (18+ verification, capacity of reflection, pre-procedure aftercare advice).
  - Digital Signature & Timestamp.

---

### 2. 💎 Piercing Consent & Release (`piercing_consent`)
- **Primary Use**: Body piercing, ear curation, surface anchors, and oral/facial piercings conforming to professional body piercing standards.
- **Key Sections**:
  - Studio & qualified professional piercer Licensing & Information.
  - Sterilization & Autoclave Lot Verification (needle lot numbers, Statim cycle logs, biological spore indicator tracking).
  - Client Health Screening (fainting history, keloid scarring tendency, bloodborne pathogen disclosures).
  - Piercing Location & Objective Material / Gauge Specifications (ASTM F-136 Implant Grade Ti6Al4V ELI Titanium, ASTM F-138 316LVM Stainless Steel, biocompatible solid gold, AWG/metric wire gauge matching).
  - Aftercare Confirmation (sterile saline spray 0.9% USP, non-touch technique) & Legal Assumption of Risks.
  - Digital Signature.

---

### 3. 🩺 Medical History & Health Screening Intake (`medical_history_intake`)
- **Primary Use**: In-depth health evaluation for high-risk clients or clinical studio procedures.
- **Key Sections**:
  - Detailed Clinical Review (cardiovascular, hemophilia, autoimmune conditions, allergies).
  - Prescription Medication Disclosures (blood thinners, immunosuppressants, Accutane).
  - Physician Authorization Declaration & Upload Field.
  - Practitioner Sign-off & Risk Assessment Notes.

---

### 4. 🎨 Large Scale Multi-Session Tattoo Project (`multisession_project`)
- **Primary Use**: Full back pieces, sleeves, bodysuits, and multi-visit projects.
- **Key Sections**:
  - Project Scope & Estimated Session Breakdown.
  - Deposit Policy, Cancellation Rules & Hourly/Day Rate Agreements.
  - Session-by-Session Healing Checkpoints & Pigment Formulas.
  - Pain Tolerance Records & Anesthetic Usage Logs.
  - Milestone Approval Signatures.

---

### 5. 🛡️ Minor Consent & Legal Guardian Authorization (`minor_consent`)
- **Primary Use**: Piercing or tattoo appointments for minors (where permitted by jurisdiction).
- **Key Sections**:
  - Minor Identification & Birth Certificate / ID Verification.
  - Parent / Legal Guardian Proof of Custody.
  - Dual Legal Disclaimers & Guardian Assumption of Responsibility.
  - Dual Digital Signatures (Minor + Parent/Guardian).

---

### 6. 🔄 Cover-Up & Tattoo Rework Assessment (`coverup_rework`)
- **Primary Use**: Tattoo rework, blast-overs, scar camouflage, and old ink coverage.
- **Key Sections**:
  - Documentation of Original Tattoo (age, raised scar tissue, laser removal history).
  - Design Limitations & Artist Creative Freedom Waiver.
  - Darker Pigment & Multiple Pass Disclosure.
  - Client Flexibility & Result Expectation Agreement.

---

### 7. 💄 PMU & Cosmetic Tattoo Consultation (`pmu_cosmetic`)
- **Primary Use**: Microblading, powder brows, lip blushing, permanent eyeliner, and scalp micropigmentation (SMP).
- **Key Sections**:
  - Facial Skin Type Analysis (oiliness, sun damage, botox/filler timeline).
  - Pigment Undertone & Shape Mapping Approval.
  - Patch Test Confirmation & Pre-Care Compliance.
  - Touch-Up Schedule & Color Retention Policies.

---

### 8. 📄 Blank Template (`blank`)
- **Primary Use**: Custom studio workflows, event waivers, guest artist forms, or unique intake surveys.
- **Starts with**: Clean canvas with ready-to-use section containers.
