# Form Builder - User Guide

## Getting Started
Welcome to the Poli International Form Builder (Tool #13). This tool allows you to create professional, legally compliant consultation forms for tattoo and piercing studios without writing any code.

### Features
- **Drag & Drop Builder**: Easily add and arrange fields.
- **Pre-Built Templates**: Start with industry-standard forms.
- **PDF Export**: Generate printable/shareable PDFs instantly.
- **Digital Signatures**: Capture client consent directly on screen.
- **Mobile Friendly**: Works on tablets and phones.

## How to Use

### 1. Choose a Template
When you open the tool, select a template from the dropdown menu in the header:
- **Standard Tattoo**: Good for most appointments.
- **Piercing**: Specialized for body piercing.
- **Large Piece**: For multi-session projects.
- **Minor Consent**: Includes guardian signature fields.

### 2. Customize Your Form
- **Add Fields**: Drag items from the Left Panel (Field Palette) onto the Center Canvas.
- **Edit Fields**: Click any field on the canvas to open the Right Panel (Properties). You can change labels, make fields required, or add placeholder text.
- **Reorder**: Drag fields up and down to change their order.
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon on a field to remove it.

### 3. Preview & Test
Click **"Preview Form"** in the top right to see how it looks to your clients. You can fill it out test data here.

### 4. Export / Share
- **PDF**: In Preview mode, fill out the form and click "Generate PDF".
- **Embed**: Click the "Embed" button to get a code snippet you can paste into your website to host the form there.

## Tips
- Always ensure the **Medical History** section is included for liability reasons.
- Use **Page Breaks** are handled automatically in the PDF, but try to keep text concise.
